    <!-- Bio -->
    <div class="mb-4">
      <p class="lead">
        Ger is a coach at Bohemian Fitness who brings empathy, lived experience, and a strong belief in the
        power of community to everything she does.
      </p>
      <p>
        She grew up in a very sporty family. Her father was one of the founders of Maynooth Athletics Club, and
        both of her parents were involved in setting up the first Dublin City Marathon. Sport was always part
        of her environment, but her own relationship with exercise took time to develop.
      </p>
      <p>
        During adolescence, Ger struggled with her weight, which affected her confidence, social life, and
        overall health. By the age of 17, she was close to 16 stone. Determined to change direction, she joined
        her local soccer team and later the GAA team, training alongside the same group of girls who would
        become a major source of support.
      </p>
      <p>
        The sense of camaraderie and belonging helped her build healthier habits and a more positive
        relationship with training. Over time, Ger completed multiple 10km races, a half marathon, and two
        mountain climbing challenges. Due to a weak Achilles and ankle, a full marathon is currently off the
        table, but still a future goal.
      </p>
      <p>
        As gym based strength and conditioning became more popular, Ger shifted her focus toward building
        physical and mental strength. She has competed in the CrossFit Open and a range of gym competitions,
        which she continues to enjoy.
      </p>
      <p>
        What drew Ger to Bohemian Fitness was the structure, coaching, and sense of overall wellbeing that
        comes from the programmed classes. Knowing she had a class booked removed mental barriers and allowed
        her to train consistently, feel supported, and move on with her day feeling accomplished.
      </p>
      <p>
        Inspired by the coaching, community, and culture of the gym, Ger decided to pursue coaching herself.
        She completed her Gym Instruction and Personal Trainer qualifications and now uses her own experiences
        to support people who are starting their journey, just as she once did.
      </p>
    </div>
